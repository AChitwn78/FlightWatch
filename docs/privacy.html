FlightWatch Privacy Policy

Effective September 30, 2026

FlightWatch does not require a FlightWatch account, contains no advertising, and does not use analytics or tracking SDKs.

Information stored on your device

Your saved flight itineraries, price history, preferences, proxy URL, and optional SerpApi key are stored in this browser's local storage. They are not synchronized to your other devices by FlightWatch. Safari may remove local data when you clear website data or delete the Home Screen app. Do not use a shared browser profile to save a private API key.

Live flight searches

When you perform a live search or refresh a tracked fare, FlightWatch sends your departure airport, arrival airport, travel dates, search preferences, and SerpApi key over HTTPS to the proxy URL you configured. The supplied proxy validates the request and forwards it to SerpApi without intentionally storing the key or search. Your proxy host may process network metadata under its own terms. SerpApi returns structured Google Flights results and processes the request under its own privacy policy and terms. FlightWatch is independent and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Google or SerpApi.

Read the SerpApi Privacy Policy

Notifications

If you grant permission, FlightWatch can create notifications for price drops detected while the web app is open and checking fares. On iPhone, install FlightWatch on the Home Screen before enabling notifications. Permission can be changed in Apple Settings.

Data sharing and sale

The developer does not sell your data. Data entered for live searches is shared with the proxy host and SerpApi only to provide the requested flight-search function.

Deleting your data

Stopping tracking removes an itinerary from the watchlist. You can remove all locally saved data, including the API key, by clearing website data for this site in Safari.

Children

FlightWatch is a general travel utility and is not directed to children under 13.

Policy changes

This policy will be updated before any release that introduces developer-operated accounts, analytics, cloud synchronization, or server-based monitoring.

Contact

For privacy questions, open an issue in the FlightWatch GitHub repository. Do not include your SerpApi key.
